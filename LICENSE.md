original installer scripts. Not affiliated with Adobe Inc.

## Hyphenation patterns — CC BY 4.0

`dictionaries/is_IS/hyph_is_IS.dic` (from `sources/hyph_is.dic`, unmodified
apart from the filename): Icelandic hyphenation rules developed by the
Icelandic Language Institute, now part of the Árni Magnússon Institute for
Icelandic Studies (Stofnun Árna Magnússonar í íslenskum fræðum), published at
malfong.is under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license
(CC BY 4.0, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). Lineage: Jörgen
Pind's TeX hyphenation patterns and Magnús Gíslason's work with Frank M.
Liang's algorithm.

## Spelling dictionary — public domain + CC BY-SA 3.0

`dictionaries/is_IS/is_IS.aff` and `is_IS.dic` (from `sources/is.aff` /
`is.dic`, unmodified apart from the filenames): generated by Hunspell-is
(https://github.com/nifgraup/hunspell-is). The underlying wordlist was
developed by Orðabók Háskólans with Reiknistofnun Háskóla Íslands and released
into the public domain; additional words and morphological information from
the Icelandic Wiktionary project are licensed CC BY-SA 3.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/).

All dictionary files were obtained via the LibreOffice dictionaries
collection: https://github.com/LibreOffice/dictionaries (directory `is/`).
The full upstream license text is preserved in `sources/license.txt` and is
installed alongside the dictionaries as `README_is_IS.txt`.
